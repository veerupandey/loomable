# India Brief — Q3 Snapshot

## Economy
India remains one of the fastest-growing major economies. Services and manufacturing continue to expand, with strong domestic demand.

## Technology
Public digital infrastructure (UPI, DigiLocker, Aadhaar-linked services) continues to scale. Startup funding is selective but resilient in AI and climate tech.

## Governance themes
Key policy conversations include infrastructure investment, manufacturing incentives, digital public goods, and regional diplomacy.
